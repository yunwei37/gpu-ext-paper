\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We presented \sys, an event-driven GPU policy interface with driver-owned transition validation and distinct host/device execution paths. Workload-specific policies improve throughput by up to $1.76\times$ over application-tuned baselines and reduce tail latency by up to $2\times$ in the original case studies. Those gains reflect policy choices. Matched-policy experiments additionally expose mechanism costs, unresolved native/BPF differences and throughput losses against simpler baselines; their execution and verification boundaries limit the claims that follow from these results.
