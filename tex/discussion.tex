\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

NVBit~\cite{villa2019nvbit} provides dynamic SASS-level instrumentation and Neutrino~\cite{neutrino} enables instruction-level profiling, both offering device-side visibility by injecting logic into GPU binaries. However, these tools lack safety guarantees (injected logic can cause crashes or deadlocks), target offline profiling rather than online policy enforcement, and cannot coordinate policies across processes or with host-side drivers. A prior workshop version~\cite{egpu} extends eBPF to GPU instrumentation but remains limited to read-only observability with high overhead (\S\ref{sec:eval}) since naively running scalar instrumentations per thread introduces warp divergence and serialization overhead on SIMT hardware. \sys builds on the eBPF-on-GPU direction but introduces SIMT-aware verification and warp-level execution to enable safe, low-overhead policy enforcement beyond read-only tracing.
